# inSitu Sales

inSitu Sales helps manufacturers, wholesalers, and distributors run field sales, van sales, and DSD operations from a conversation with Claude. Find customers and products; review pricing, inventory, visits, orders, estimates, and invoices; generate customer statements; get order recommendations; and create or update sales records.

inSitu Sales connects with ERP and accounting systems, including QuickBooks Enterprise, QuickBooks Online, Odoo, NetSuite, SAP Business One, Xero, and Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central, so sales and operational data come together in one workflow.

## Use it

1. Add the **inSitu Sales** plugin.
2. Connect the **inSitu Sales** connector and sign in with your existing inSitu Sales credentials. Until you sign in, the connector may show as disconnected.
3. Ask Claude about your sales data, for example:
   - "Show me today's invoices"
   - "What were my top-selling products last week?"
   - "Create an order for this customer with last month's items"

You need an inSitu Sales account. Each organization sees only the business data and workflows its users are authorized to access.

## Data

The plugin connects Claude to the inSitu Sales MCP server at `https://api.insitusales.com/mcp` over HTTPS. Your requests, and the customer, product, and sales records needed to answer them, are exchanged with your inSitu Sales account through that server. Sign-in uses OAuth with your inSitu Sales credentials. The plugin itself contains no scripts and stores no data on your device.

See the inSitu Sales privacy policy and terms: https://www.insitusales.com/end-user-license-agreement
